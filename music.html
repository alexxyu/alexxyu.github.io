Music

Hey there! If you're interested in learning a little more about me, this page is dedicated to my experience with music, especially as a cellist.

I began learning piano at five years old but eventually quit to pursue cello as my main instrument. I don't regret this decision as cello has opened so many doors for me, but I still sometimes play piano when I have the chance. Recently, I started teaching myself how to play guitar.

Experience
  • Member of the UCLA Symphony Orchestra from 2019 to 2023
  • Member of the Harker School Orchestra from 2015 to 2019; Served as assistant principal for 2018-19 season
  • Member of Harker School string quartets and cello ensembles
  • Studied under Jonathan Koh from 2013 to 2019 and Irene Sharp from 2010 to 2013
