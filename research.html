Research Experience

I'm currently a research assistant in the Zili Lab at UCLA, working on psychology-releated problems.
I'm especially interested in research related to artificial intelligence and computational biology.

Here are a few projects I've worked on:

Analysis of the aging process in yeast cells associated with the cell cycle regulator WHI5 (tagged with GFP)
Analysis of proteomic and physical differences that may account for great lifespan variation between mammalian species
Use of support vector machines (SVMs) to analyze whether the middle temporal cortex can process opposite motion signals
Simulation of decision-rule models that may explain how humans respond in a same-different experiment
Study of the motion aftereffect (MAE) and its consequences on visual discrimination sensitivity between directional stimuli
