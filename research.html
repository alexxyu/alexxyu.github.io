Research Experience

I've previously worked as a research assistant for the Zili Lab at UCLA and the Li Lab at UCSF. I'm particularly interested in research related to artificial intelligence and computational biology.

Here are a few projects I've worked on:

Analysis of the aging process in yeast cells associated with the cell cycle regulator WHI5 (tagged with GFP)

Analysis of proteomic and physical differences that may account for variation in lifespan between mammalian species

Use of support vector machines to analyze motion opponency directional information in the MT cortex

Study of several decision-rule models that may explain human responses in a same-different experiment

Study of the motion aftereffect (MAE) and its consequences on visual discrimination sensitivity between directional stimuli
